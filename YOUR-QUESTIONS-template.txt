-----------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE SCORING WORKS (so you can weight your own answers)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Every answer carries a number from 0 to 3:

  0 = healthy / settled / no concern
  1 = mild
  2 = moderate
  3 = strong indicator

The numbers add up across all questions, and the total decides
which ending she sees.

You do NOT have to fill these in. If you'd rather, just write the
questions and answers and I'll assign sensible numbers, then you
can tell me if any feel off.

Answers per question: 4 works nicely, but 3 or 5 is fine too, and
they don't all have to have the same number.
